Integrations

Integrations are chosen when you install. Tick them in the app, or pass them as options to the unfr0zen command-line tool: on Linux and macOS, the app's own executable run with arguments. Windows has the app only. To add or remove one later, run the install again with the new selection. MQTT, Matter, Zigbee and Doom are served by nestlocal, which any of them installs on the thermostat.

MQTT and Matter reach your network over Wi-Fi: join it from the thermostat's own menu (Settings → Network). That network doesn't need internet access.

UNFR0ZEN menu

The install replaces the thermostat's NEST APP settings entry with UNFR0ZEN. It shows the Matter QR and pairing code with Enable pairing, Zigbee pairing with a 30-second countdown, and Doom. An integration's entry appears only when it was installed.

The original nlclient is saved once on the unit, at /etc/unfr0zen/nlclient.original, and kept across later installs. No prebuilt binary patches are used: the app links the menu into the unit's own nlclient. Supported versions and undo: nlclient/README.md.

MQTT

AppCLI
Relay to MQTT broker, Broker host[:port]--mqtt host[:port]
User, Password--mqtt-user, --mqtt-password
TLS, CA certificate--mqtt-tls, --mqtt-ca FILE

The port defaults to 1883, or 8883 with TLS. The thermostat shows up in Home Assistant through MQTT discovery: modes, setpoints and range, what it is running, eco and the eco temperatures, humidity and target humidity, outdoor temperature, occupancy, °C/°F, backplate temperature, battery, fan and its timer, Auto-Schedule, Home/Away Assist, Sunblock, Early-On, the indicators (Leaf, Early-On and Sunblock active, safety shutoff, filter), time to temperature, IP address, Wi-Fi signal and clearing the schedule.

Two Home Assistant blueprints feed the thermostat what it used to get from Google:

  • Nest weather: outdoor temperature, humidity, conditions and forecast, every 15 minutes.
  • Nest clock: answers the thermostat's time requests on a network with no NTP server.

Matter

AppCLI
Matter--matter
Pairing code--matter-code CODE

Works alone or beside MQTT. The thermostat must already be on Wi-Fi. It is commissioned with its 11-digit pairing code, 0347-100-5534 unless you set another. The first pairing window opens by itself; after that, open UNFR0ZEN → Matter → Enable pairing.

  1. Home Assistant: Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Matter.
  2. Add the device with the pairing code, or scan the QR shown on the thermostat.
nestlocal uses the Matter test vendor ID (0xFFF1) and test attestation, so controllers treat it as uncertified. Home Assistant's Matter server accepts it only with its test-net DCL option on.

Home Assistant shows the thermostat (modes, setpoints and range, what it is running, eco as a preset, humidity, outdoor temperature), occupancy, °C/°F, backplate temperature, battery, fan and its timer, and switches for Auto-Schedule, Home/Away Assist, Sunblock, Early-On and the eco limits. The rest of the MQTT entities are MQTT-only.

Bluetooth commissioning. On units with the supported DA14580 Bluetooth firmware, Matter also commissions over Bluetooth, automatically. The stock radio image stays unchanged: nestlocal loads the unit's own firmware into radio RAM. Bluetooth advertises only while the pairing window is open; day-to-day traffic goes over Wi-Fi or Thread. Units with missing or unsupported Bluetooth firmware pair over IP.

Sleep. The thermostat sleeps between Matter commands: unicast UDP to port 5540 wakes it. It stays awake while commissioning, and on firmware that rejects the wake filters. Multicast discovery and subscription deadlines don't wake it yet. This is experimental. On the 2nd generation, Matter keeps the unit awake: its sleep daemon wakes only for TCP traffic.

Matter over Thread

AppCLI
Thread support, under Matter--matter-thread

The thermostat joins a Thread mesh as a router and serves Matter over it, beside Wi-Fi. The commissioner supplies the Thread credentials. It takes over the radio from Nest's Thread firmware (Heat Link), so it can't be combined with Zigbee. Experimental, and untested on a 2nd generation unit, whose radio holds one firmware at a time: nestlocal flashes a Thread image for it. Design and device details: radio/thread/README.md.

Zigbee

AppCLI
Zigbee--zigbee

The thermostat joins your Zigbee network as a router through its own 802.15.4 radio, alone or beside MQTT and Matter. On first start nestlocal writes its own radio firmware in place of Nest's Thread firmware (Heat Link). Installing again without Zigbee puts Nest's firmware back. On the 2nd generation, Zigbee is untested on a unit and transmits at Nest's -10 dBm limit.

  1. Zigbee2MQTT 2.12.1 or later: copy nest-learning-thermostat.mjs to data/external_converters/ and restart Zigbee2MQTT.
  2. Permit join on the coordinator. The thermostat joins the strongest network permitting joins, on any channel, and keeps it across restarts.
  3. After updating the converter, Reconfigure the device in Zigbee2MQTT so Home Assistant gets the new entities.

Home Assistant gets the MQTT entities under the same names, except the IP address, Wi-Fi signal and the blueprints.

To move to another network, open UNFR0ZEN → Zigbee → Enable pairing and confirm. The thermostat beeps, leaves the network, forgets its credentials and looks for one permitting joins for 30 seconds. After a timeout it stays off any network until pairing is enabled again or nestlocal restarts.

The thermostat still sleeps: the radio keeps routing, and a message for the unit wakes it. The wake comes from a small kernel module built for each gen3 kernel Nest published (5.8 to 6.1). On any other kernel, a message waits until the unit wakes for something else.

Doom

AppCLI
Not available--doom

Installs Doom with the shareware episode (DOOM1.WAD v1.9, included). Start it from UNFR0ZEN → Doom.

  • You walk forward all along. Turn the ring to steer, press it to fire. Doors and switches open as you walk into them.
  • In menus, the ring moves and a press selects.
  • Hold the ring for 3 seconds to quit back to the thermostat.
  • Sound comes from the piezo: the PC speaker effects and each song's highest note.
While Doom runs the thermostat does not control the heating or cooling.

Backup & restore

AppCLI
Back up NANDunfr0zen backup [OUT]
Not availableunfr0zen verify IMAGE
Restore NANDunfr0zen restore IMAGE

Backs up the whole NAND as an image, checked with a second read, with a .sha256 beside it: 512 MiB ECC-corrected on the 3rd generation, raw pages with their OOB on the 2nd. Nothing is written to the NAND during a backup, and an interrupted backup resumes. Restore writes a verified image back and asks first. On the 2nd generation, the image's factory-bad blocks must match the chip's, only blocks that differ are rewritten, and an interrupted restore is run again.
