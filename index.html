No cloud

No Google account, no internet needed. The thermostat talks to your own network only, and the Wi-Fi it joins doesn't need internet access.

MQTT

Relays the thermostat to your MQTT broker with Home Assistant's MQTT discovery: modes, setpoints, eco, humidity, fan, schedule and more.

Matter

Serves the unit as a Matter thermostat over Wi-Fi, commissioned with a pairing code or over Bluetooth on supported units. Thread support is experimental.

Zigbee

Joins your Zigbee2MQTT network as a router through the thermostat's own 802.15.4 radio, and still sleeps between messages.

On-device settings

An UNFR0ZEN menu on the thermostat itself shows Matter pairing codes and QR, and handles Zigbee pairing. The original firmware is kept for undo.

One app, every OS

A single self-contained executable for Windows, macOS and Linux. Recovery firmware is embedded; plug in a data micro-USB cable and go.

Backup & restore

The app and the CLI back up and restore the thermostat's whole NAND as an image before you touch anything.

It runs Doom

Obviously. Turn the ring to walk, press to fire. The shareware episode is included.

Open source

The app, the recovery firmware, nestlocal and the radio firmware are all built from source. No prebuilt binary patches.
